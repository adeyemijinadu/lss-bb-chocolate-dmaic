| Field | Detail |

| Project title |Improving Cupping Quality Consistency in Green Coffee Bean Intake. |
| Problem statement |Of the coffee bean lots received from suppliers between 2010 and 2018, approximately 14% (13.8%) score below 80 points on the Total Cup Points scale — the Specialty Coffee Association's threshold for specialty-grade classification. Lots scoring below this threshold are treated as commodity-grade and sold at a lower price than specialty-grade lots would command, representing an estimated loss of approximately $739,000 over this period (based on a sourced price gap of $1.06/kg between average specialty ($7.50/kg) and commodity ($6.44/kg) green coffee prices). Beyond the direct financial impact, receiving and processing sub-specialty-grade lots as if they were specialty-grade exposes the company to reputational and customer-trust risk. |
| Goal statement |Upon implementation, reduce the rate of coffee bean lots scoring below 80 points on the Total Cup Points scale from the current baseline of 13.8% to 5% or below, within 6 months of rollout. |
